# Marketplace Digital Universal / Universal Digital Marketplace

## Canonical category

The product category is **Marketplace Digital Universal**; the English internal name is **Universal Digital Marketplace**.

Product thesis: **Convertimos el trabajo digital en algo que simplemente puedes pedir.**

Commercial thesis: **The customer is buying back time, attention and execution risk.**

Supporting positioning:

- **Compra lo que necesitas hecho.**
- **No te vendemos una herramienta para que hagas el trabajo. Te vendemos el trabajo digital terminado.**
- **Cada producto tiene una fórmula versionada. Cada pedido genera una especificación. Cada entrega debe demostrar que cumple esa especificación.**

These are `[HYPOTHESIS]` positioning statements. The repository currently proves narrow technical controls over Precision Edit; it does not prove marketplace demand or superiority over every DIY, AI, agency, freelancer, or internal-build alternative.

## What the customer buys

The unit is a finished digital Outcome: a deliverable with explicit requirements, provenance, evidence, review, and controlled delivery. It is not access to a model, a prompt box, or a promise that one generation is automatically complete.

The customer may be technical or non-technical. Lack of AI skill is only one possible reason to buy. The broader value is recovering scarce time and attention while transferring bounded execution and quality risk to a specialized, versioned production formula.

Persona value hypotheses:

- Developer: buys back engineering time spent on non-differentiating work.
- Designer: buys back production and iteration time.
- Editor: buys back repetitive execution and technical QA time.
- Automation specialist: buys back commodity integration time.
- Company: reduces time-to-outcome and delivery risk.
- Non-technical buyer: receives a result without first acquiring tool expertise.

The cross-skill marketplace advantage is `[HYPOTHESIS]`: professionals may buy Outcomes outside their specialty instead of learning and maintaining every adjacent toolchain.

## Discovery

The planned discovery model is **Product + Problem + Project**:

- Product: a directly discoverable Outcome SKU.
- Problem: language describing what the buyer needs solved.
- Project: a tenant-owned planning context containing Outcome needs and accepted transaction references.

A future Digital Solution Graph may describe curated or evidence-supported relationships among products and needs. This is a `[HYPOTHESIS]`, not a graph database commitment. Relevance may include controlled serendipity, but recommendations must never invent trust, acceptance, or verification claims.

Customer/Business Canon is planned, versioned context. Every entry retains provenance. `INFERRED` data cannot silently become `APPROVED`, and Project/Canon context cannot mutate canonical assets outside normal Outcome transactions.

## Messaging guardrails

Do not say:

- every generic AI requires a subscription;
- developers or designers cannot build these products;
- generic AI always produces poor quality;
- our Outcomes are always better;
- “verified” without naming the check and evidence.

Prefer:

- save time and reduce trial-and-error;
- buy back scarce attention;
- receive a finished, verified deliverable;
- use a specialized production formula;
- focus internal effort on differentiated work.

## Launch boundary

The architecture is universal; the implementation remains narrow. Precision Edit is the only deterministic compiler proof. Public marketplace UI, seller payments, recommendation infrastructure, arbitrary seller code, Creator Studio, and BUILD 006 are not authorized by this positioning.
